# Mining pains

Use this only when there is no customer and no job yet. If the user already named either, stop and follow `clarifying-the-idea`.

Read `skills/pitching-an-idea/references/claim-rules.md`, `skills/pitching-an-idea/references/genres.md`, and `skills/pitching-an-idea/references/artifact-layout.md`.

## Before search

Need a genre from `genres.md`, or a place (a city, a trade, a tool people already use). If both are missing, ask one question and wait. Do not search the whole internet for "startup ideas."

## Search

Live web search only. Look for complaints, reviews, "how do you handle" threads, and job posts inside that genre or place. If search is unavailable, stop. Do not invent pains from memory.

Write 5 to 8 candidates in `pitches/<slug>/pains.md`. The slug can be the genre or the place. Each candidate is one job, not a company name.

```markdown
# Pains

- Genre or place:
- Searched: YYYY-MM-DD

## 1. <job in a few words>
- One sentence: who, and what they are trying to finish
- Genre:
- Complaint: paraphrase, FACT, URL
- Dies because:
- Evidence that would save it:

## Pick
Waiting on the user. Do not mark a winner.
```

Every complaint line has a URL you opened. If a query returns nothing, write the query and "none found," and do not fill the gap with a candidate.

Stop. Ask which job to carry forward. Do not pick. Do not draft.

When they pick, follow `clarifying-the-idea` and set the idea, customer, and job from that candidate. Leave the other candidates in the file.
