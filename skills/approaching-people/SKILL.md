# Approaching people

Write `pitches/<slug>/approach.md`. Do not send email, DMs, or posts. Do not scrape addresses. Do not produce a list of recipients.

Read `brief.md`, `people.md`, `critique.md`, `skills/pitching-an-idea/references/claim-rules.md`, and `skills/pitching-an-idea/references/genres.md`. If `people.md` or `critique.md` is missing, stop and name the missing file.

Copy the Pass and Fail lines from `critique.md`. If the genre has a test override in `genres.md`, those lines are already the ones that belong in the critique. Do not invent a looser test.

```markdown
# Approach

Nothing in this file has been sent.

## Who to talk to
10 conversations unless the genre override says otherwise.
- 6 who did the job in the last month
- 2 who switched or gave up
- 1 neighbor segment
- 1 extra, for a no-show

Use names from people.md when they exist. Leave a slot blank rather than inventing a person.

## The ask
One note for one named person. Fill the brackets from the brief. Keep it under 80 words.

> Hi <name>, I'm researching how <role> handles <job from the brief>, especially the last time it happened. I'm not selling anything. Would you give me 15 minutes this week so I can learn what you do today? I'll send you a short summary of what I hear from the others.

## Follow-up
One note, sent only if they do not reply. Then stop.

> Hi <name>, bringing this back to the top in case it was buried. Still not selling. 15 minutes on how you handle <job> would help. Fine if now is a bad time.

## In a room
Answer the person's question in the thread first. Mention what you are building only when it is the honest answer, and say that you built it. Read the rules in people.md before posting. If the rules are UNKNOWN, do not post.

## The call
Open: "I'm trying to understand how you deal with <job>. I won't pitch. May I take notes?"
Then, in their words: when it last happened, what they did, what it cost, what they already paid for, what they did after that.
If you describe the product, stop. You have left the interview.

## Pass and fail
Copy from critique.md.
```

## When they push

| Excuse | What you do instead |
| --- | --- |
| Email 50 or 200 of them | One note. No recipient list. |
| Find their emails | Stop. No addresses in the file. |
| Send it for me | Leave the note in the file. Say it was not sent. |
| Pitch them on the call so it is faster | The call script stays on their last time, not your product. |
