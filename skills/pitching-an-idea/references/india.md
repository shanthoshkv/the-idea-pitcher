# India

Default market is India. Read this when `Market` on the brief is India or any Indian city or state. Everything here is a search hint or a rule. None of it is a FACT until a page you opened says so.

## Money

- Write rupees as ₹ with lakh and crore: ₹5,000, ₹2.4 lakh, ₹18 crore. 1 lakh = 100,000. 1 crore = 100 lakh.
- A USD figure from a source stays in USD with its source. If you convert, tag the result INFERENCE and name the rate, its source, and the date.
- Prices are monthly unless the brief says otherwise. Say whether GST is included.
- Low price per customer is the default risk. The critic asks how one customer is acquired for less than that customer pays over their first year. If the brief cannot answer, that is an assumption in `critique.md`.

## Where Indian claims come from

An Indian market, price, funding, or user-count claim needs an Indian source, or a global source that breaks out India. A US or global figure is not an India figure.

Search hints, in rough order of trust:

| Kind of claim | Look here first |
| --- | --- |
| Official counts, surveys, census, MSME, trade | MOSPI, data.gov.in, Udyam / MSME ministry, DGCI&S, the relevant ministry's annual report |
| Payments, lending, banking | RBI publications, NPCI monthly UPI statistics |
| Telecom and internet users | TRAI reports |
| Company existence, filings, directors | MCA21 company master data |
| Startup recognition counts, schemes | DPIIT / Startup India portal |
| Funding rounds, competitors | Inc42, Entrackr, YourStory, The Ken, Economic Times, Mint, Tracxn or Crunchbase public pages |
| Listed company numbers | Annual reports and exchange filings on BSE or NSE |

A news article quoting a consulting report is a FACT about the article. Find the report, or tag the number with the article as publisher.

## Regulators to check by genre

Search hints for `checking-regulation`. Confirm each on the regulator's own site.

| If the idea touches | Check |
| --- | --- |
| Payments, wallets, lending, credit data | RBI (payment aggregator, NBFC, digital lending), NPCI rails |
| Investments, advice, trading | SEBI (investment adviser, research analyst, broker) |
| Insurance | IRDAI |
| Food, packaged or delivered | FSSAI licence or registration |
| Drugs, devices, diagnostics | CDSCO, state drug controller |
| Telemedicine, health records | Telemedicine practice guidelines, ABDM |
| Personal data of any user | Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules |
| Selling goods or services | GST registration, Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules |
| Ads and claims | ASCI code |
| Education, coaching | State coaching rules, UGC / AICTE if credits are offered |
| Drones, space, defence | DGCA, IN-SPACe, iDEX |

A licence the idea needs is never optional. A licence that takes months is a constraint on the brief.

## How to split the customer

A customer is not "India" or "Bharat". Add at least two of these to the brief:

- Place: a named city or district, and its tier (metro, Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3, rural).
- Language they buy in: English, Hindi, Hinglish, or a named regional language.
- How they pay: UPI, cash on delivery, card, EMI, credit from the seller, employer pays.
- Device: smartphone only, shared phone, laptop at work, feature phone.
- Where they already transact: WhatsApp, a marketplace, a kirana, a distributor, a dealer, a government portal.

A metro result is not a Tier 2 result. That leap is an ASSUMPTION.

## Rooms

Kinds of places Indian customers already talk. Use as search terms only. A room needs a URL you opened.

- WhatsApp and Telegram groups linked from public pages
- LinkedIn posts and comments by the job title
- Subreddits for an Indian city, profession, or exam
- Trade, trader, dealer, and resident associations
- College E-cells, alumni groups, professional bodies (ICAI, IMA, bar associations)
- YouTube comments under videos about the job in the customer's language

## Funding and support

Search hints for `finding-investors` and for the `grant` and `incubator` audiences. Eligibility and amounts change. Quote them only from the scheme's own page.

- Central schemes: Startup India Seed Fund Scheme, BIRAC BIG (biotech), iDEX (defence), MeitY TIDE / SAMRIDH, DST NIDHI
- State schemes: Karnataka Elevate, KSUM (Kerala), T-Hub and T-Works (Telangana), Startup TN, iStart Rajasthan, Gujarat SSIP
- Incubators: university and IIM / IIT incubators, such as NSRCEL, CIIE.CO, SINE, IIT Madras Incubation Cell
- Angels and early funds: public portfolio pages, Inc42 and Entrackr funding news, angel network sites

## Objections the critic asks in India

Add these to the strongest-objection pool when the market is India. Each needs evidence from the files, or it stays an open question.

- A large incumbent (Reliance, Tata, Flipkart, Zomato, PhonePe, Paytm) can bundle this for free. What stops them?
- The jugaad already works. The WhatsApp group, the munshi, the relative, the Excel sheet. Why switch?
- Willingness to pay. Who has paid for anything like this before, and how much?
- Metro-only. Does the job exist the same way outside the founder's city?
- Collection. Will the buyer pay on time, and who chases them?
- Trust. Why would this customer give money or data to a new company?
