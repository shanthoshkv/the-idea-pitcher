# Pitch shapes

Read `audience` from the brief. Same facts, different front door. If `audience` is missing, stop and ask. Do not assume investor.

| Audience | The ask |
| --- | --- |
| investor | Amount, use of funds, and the milestone the money buys |
| cofounder | The role, what is already true, and the risk they take |
| customer | The pain in their words, and what changes the day they switch |
| grant | Who is helped, the public outcome, and what the funds produce |

Facts come from `brief.md`, `research.md`, and `critique.md`. If a number is not tagged there, omit it or mark it UNKNOWN.

Read `genres.md`. The problem and competition sections answer the proof line for the genre on the brief.

## Spoken

Michael Seibel's seven questions, in this order. At most two sentences each. Traction of zero is said as zero.

1. What you do. Name, then a concrete picture a stranger can repeat. No warm-up.
2. Market. Bottom-up, or UNKNOWN. No unsourced giant number.
3. Traction. Founder-supplied only.
4. Insight. The non-obvious belief. ASSUMPTION unless sourced.
5. Money. Who pays, for what, at what price. UNKNOWN if unset.
6. Team. Only what the founder said. Blank if they said nothing. No invented advisors.
7. The ask. Match the audience table.

## Memo

One to two pages. This order:

1. Purpose. One declarative sentence.
2. Problem. The pain, and how they cope today.
3. Solution. What it does, and the change for that customer.
4. Why now. From `research.md`. If the research calls it weak, say weak.
5. Market. Bottom-up. A top-down FACT may sit beside it, with publisher and year.
6. Competition and alternatives. Include the status quo.
7. Business model.
8. Team.
9. The ask and the milestone.

No profit-and-loss, balance sheet, or cap table.

## Deck outline

10 to 14 slides. One claim per slide. Order follows the memo. For each slide write:

- Title. The claim itself, not a label such as "Market".
- On the slide. One sentence, or three short lines.
- Speaker note. Two sentences.
- Tags. The claim tags used.

This is an outline, not a designed deck. No appendix.
