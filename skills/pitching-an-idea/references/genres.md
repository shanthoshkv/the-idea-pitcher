# Genres

Set `Genre` on the brief to one of these names. If two fit, ask once. Do not add a genre.

| Genre | The pitch has to prove | Where to search for rooms | Fatal if missing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| vertical-software | A named buyer who already pays to do this job badly | The job title, a trade group, job posts for the role they would stop hiring | No named buyer |
| developer-tool | A workflow developers already complain about in public | Issue trackers, docs forums, and discussion threads for that workflow | No public complaint of the workflow |
| marketplace | Which side is harder, and why that side shows up first | Where supply already gathers, and the buyer's current order channel | Both sides required on day one, with no first side |
| consumer-app | A repeated moment, and a way to reach that person without ads | The place they already discuss that moment | The customer is a feeling or a demographic |
| services-first | A paid engagement that can be delivered before a product exists | People who already hire that service, including past coworkers | No offer a buyer could pay for this month |
| regulated | Who holds the license, how long the sale takes, and what must not be done yet | Associations, and the job title that already pays a lawyer or consultant | A license or a required partner treated as optional |
| local | A city, and enough density to walk or call | Local groups, and the place they already buy the substitute | A country-sized market for a shop-level job |
| deep-tech | Whether the risk is the science or the buyer, and who pays for a pilot | Labs, industry buyers, and grant calls | A science claim with no source, or no pilot buyer |

Room names come from search. This table is a search hint, not a directory.

## Founder fit

`Reach` on the brief is one of: `already-in-the-room`, `can-be-introduced`, `starting-from-zero`.

If Reach is `starting-from-zero` and the genre is `regulated` or `local`, the critic marks that assumption high-deadly and high-untested.

## Test overrides

The default pass and fail lines live in `criticizing-the-pitch`. Use these instead when the genre matches:

- marketplace: at least 3 supply-side and 3 demand-side conversations, plus a named first side. A pass on only one side fails.
- regulated: at least one buyer who already pays a lawyer, consultant, or compliance hire for this job.
- services-first: one person willing to pay for the manual version. The count is 1.
- local: every conversation is in the city on the brief. A conversation from another city does not count.
