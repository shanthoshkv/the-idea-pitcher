# Constable, Talking to Humans

Not stored. Giff Constable, *Talking to Humans* (2014), with Frank Rimalovski. The public free PDF is no longer on the author's site. The site now sells the book and limits free copies to nonprofits and schools. Do not save a copy from anywhere else.

The interview method this book is known for is already in `approaching-people` and `clarifying-the-idea`: leave the building, ask about the last time, do not pitch, do not ask people to design the product. No separate skill.
