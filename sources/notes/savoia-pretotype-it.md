# Savoia, Pretotype It (2011)

Full file: `../pretotype-it-savoia-2011.pdf`. Author-distributed free PDF. Copyright Alberto Savoia 2011. Source: https://www.pretotyping.org/uploads/1/4/0/9/14099067/pretotype_it_2nd_pretotype_edition-2.pdf

This edition does not use the later labels XYZ, hypozoom, or YODA. Do not add them as if they were in this file.

What the skill uses:

- Build the right thing before building it the right way. A pretotype asks whether people will act. A prototype asks whether we can build it.
- Thoughtland is opinions. Data is what people do. Most new ideas fail, so a room full of praise is not a pass.
- Techniques in this edition: Mechanical Turk (a person does the unbuilt job), Pinocchio (a non-working object treated as real), Stripped (a working minimum), Provincial (one small place), Fake Door (an entry to something that does not exist yet), Pretend-to-Own (rent or borrow before buying), Re-label (an existing product under the new offer).
- Initial interest is actions taken divided by opportunities offered. Ongoing interest is whether the same people come back. Compare two groups when you can. One percentage with no comparison is a judgment, not a proof.
- If someone would pay, travel, or hand over work for a thing that is not real, tell them first.

The testing skill owns the procedure. This note is the citation.
