# Eisenmann, Why Startups Fail

The book (2021) and the Harvard Business Review article are not stored. The six names below are the framework he uses, checked against public summaries rather than a saved chapter:

- https://hbr.org/2021/05/why-start-ups-fail
- https://www.nfx.com/post/hidden-patterns-startup-failure
- https://www.thediligentobserver.com/p/6-reasons-why-startups-fail

Early: good idea / bad bedfellows; false start; false positive. Later: speed trap; help wanted; cascading miracles. "Ran out of cash" is how a company ends, not which pattern killed it.

The failure-check skill owns the checklist. It does not retell his cases and it does not score.
