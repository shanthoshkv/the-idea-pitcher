# Graham, How to Get Startup Ideas

Not stored. Copyright Paul Graham. Read at https://paulgraham.com/startupideas.html (November 2012).

What mining-pains uses:

- Notice a problem, preferably one the founder has, instead of inventing a company.
- Prefer a few people who want it badly, including a bad first version, over many people who might mildly want it.
- A crowded category is a usable signal when you can say what the current tools skip.
- A tedious or unfashionable job is a reason to look, not a reason to skip.
- An idea that only sounds plausible, with nobody who wants it now, is a weak candidate.

"Do Things that Don't Scale" (https://paulgraham.com/ds.html) was not used. A person doing the job by hand is already in the pretotype notes.
