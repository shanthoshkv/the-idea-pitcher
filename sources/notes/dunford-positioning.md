Not stored. Copyright April Dunford. Read at https://www.aprildunford.com/post/a-quickstart-guide-to-positioning (15 March 2021). The book *Obviously Awesome* is not stored.

What the skill uses:

- Positioning says how the offer leads at something a defined set of customers cares about a lot. It is not a tagline, a brand story, or a vision.
- Do not start from a fill-in-the-blank positioning sentence. The components depend on each other, in this order only: alternatives the customer would use if you did not exist (including doing nothing); capabilities those alternatives lack; the value of those capabilities; the customers who care a lot; the category that makes that value obvious.
- An alternative is what the buyer actually does. A company they never meet in a deal is a phantom and weakens the position.
- Default category is one people already know, narrowed. A new word has to be taught before it can position anything.

Her enterprise-software remark about lost deals is her observation. Do not copy that percentage into a pitch as a fact about this idea.
