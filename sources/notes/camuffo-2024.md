# Camuffo et al. 2024

Full file: `../camuffo-2024-scientific-approach.pdf`. CC BY 4.0. DOI https://doi.org/10.1002/smj.3580

Four randomized trials, 759 firms, replicating Camuffo, Cordova, Gambardella, and Spina (2020) in *Management Science*. The 2020 paper is not stored.

What the skill uses, as a way of deciding, not as a statistic to drop into a pitch:

- Frame the problem as a theory: which attributes matter, how they cause the outcome, and how sure you are.
- Turn that theory into a prediction. Test the prediction. Update from the result. Then continue, change, or stop.
- Two reasons it helps: you know what you are searching for, and you look for the way the idea is wrong (methodic doubt).
- Stopping a weak idea is a successful use of the method. A change is focused: the value proposition or the customer, once, after a failed prediction. Endless changes are not the method.

Do not cite the trials as evidence that this founder's market is large, or that this idea will work.
