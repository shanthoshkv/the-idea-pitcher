# Sources

These files are the reading behind the testing, positioning, and failure-check skills. A full file is here only when the author or the license lets us keep it. Everything else is a short note in `notes/` plus the URL. The notes are our method sketches. They are not the articles.

| File | What it is | Why it is here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pretotype-it-savoia-2011.pdf` | Alberto Savoia, *Pretotype It*, second pretotype edition, October 2011. Copyright Alberto Savoia. | The author distributes this PDF for free. |
| `camuffo-2024-scientific-approach.pdf` | Camuffo, Gambardella, Messinese, Novelli, Paolucci, and Spina, "A scientific approach to entrepreneurial decision-making: Large-scale replication and extension," *Strategic Management Journal* 45(6): 1209–1237 (2024). DOI [10.1002/smj.3580](https://doi.org/10.1002/smj.3580). | CC BY 4.0. This copy is the published version from [City Research Online, eprint 32437](https://openaccess.city.ac.uk/id/eprint/32437/). |
| `notes/` | Original notes on Dunford, Graham, Eisenmann, Constable, and the two files above. | Copyrighted essays and books are not stored. |

The MIT license at the repo root covers the plugin. It does not cover these two PDFs. The Camuffo paper is CC BY 4.0. *Pretotype It* remains copyright Alberto Savoia.

Do not paste these sources into a pitch. A study of other founders is not a fact about this idea.
